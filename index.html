Usage

How to consume

  1. Take 1 spoon (15 ml) of ZivaGo Sea Buckthorn Juice.
  2. Mix with 150–200 ml of water.
  3. Stir well and drink once a day.

Best time: morning or whenever suits you. Suitable for adults. Use regularly as part of your daily routine.

Shake well before use. Refrigerate after opening and consume within 7 days.
